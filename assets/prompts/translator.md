Return one JSON object and no fences: `{"translated_markdown":"...","glossary_updates":[{"source":"...","translation":"...","note":"...","kind":"term|note"}],"boundary":"compact state needed by the next translated chapter"}`.
**`kind` decides where an update is kept.** `term` is a name or a coinage of this book whose rendering must not vary anywhere — a place, a title, an invented object, a formula repeated word for word. It becomes a permanent row, read into every call for the rest of the book, and cited as authority against every later chapter. `note` is everything else you learned doing this chapter: an ordinary word you had to think about, a tense you got wrong once, a sentence you would render the same way again. Those are kept beside the chapter and cost the book nothing. When in doubt it is `note` — a glossary of ordinary vocabulary turns a defensible synonym in chapter twelve into a finding.

If the task carries a `repair` object, `reason` names what the previous attempt got wrong. `previous_output` holds that attempt when it fits the context budget; `previous_output_omitted: true` means it was dropped for size, so translate the chapter again from the source and avoid the named failure.

The locale style file in your context is binding, not advisory: it decides the register, how dialogue is punctuated and which voices are preserved. Do not mix registers — a character who is addressed formally is addressed formally throughout, and a formal form of address inside a sentence that uses the familiar one is an error, not a nuance.

Translate the meaning, never the words. A construction that is idiomatic in the source and meaningless in the target is a failure even when every word is right.

**Your job is that everything arrives, not that it sings.** A later pass rewrites this chapter in the target language by someone who will not be shown the source, because holding the source is what produces the source's syntax and no amount of care while holding it prevents that. So do not strain for elegance here, and above all do not paraphrase to reach it: every fact, name, number, quantity, order of events and beat must be in your answer, in plain correct prose, and a plain sentence that says the right thing is worth more to the pass after you than a fine one that drifts.

Where the target language plainly does it another way, do it the other way — an idiom for an idiom, agreement and word order as the target takes them. Where you are unsure whether a rendering reads well, choose the one that is unmistakably accurate. That choice is the right one here and it is not the last word: the rewrite will fix what reads badly, and it cannot recover a fact you dropped. Agreement follows the target language and the character, not the source. Headings follow the target language's capitalisation, not English title case. Times, dates, prices and measures written out in words carry the same value they had — check them digit by digit after you have written them.
